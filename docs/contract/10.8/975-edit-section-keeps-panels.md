- **`crates/knobas-source/src/**` (doc comments only) and the IPC answer of `docs_page` and `docs_edit_section` — issue #975 (2026-10-17): *Edit section* keeps a panel.** Spec #808, *Docs*; requirement R-10.13; milestone TF4. Amends #860–#862's entry (`860-862-docs-search-rail-and-writes.md`) for which sections are editable and what the editor's text holds. No shape changes: no new field, variant, command, event, `WriteOp`, migration or module.

  **What is editable.** A section whose body is paragraphs, line breaks, flat bulleted lists **and info, note, warning or tip panels** (`ac:structured-macro` with `ac:name` one of those four) at the top of the section. Such a panel is editable when it carries nothing but an optional `title` parameter and an `ac:rich-text-body` that follows the section's own rules. A panel with any other parameter (`icon`, say), the generic `panel` macro (its colours and borders are parameters), a panel inside a list item and a panel inside a panel still refuse as `macro`; formatting inside a panel refuses as `formatting`. A block that is not a panel but whose text holds a line that would open one (`> **Info** …`) refuses as `formatting`, so saving unchanged never turns words into a panel.

  **`DocSection.text`** spells such a panel as the Markdown export does (`knobas_core::markdown`): a quote whose first line is the kind in bold and the title beside it, then a `>` line, then the body, every line prefixed `> ` (a blank line inside it is `>`). For example `> **Info** Before you deploy\n>\n> Drain the queue.\n> - then deploy`.

  **`DocWrites::edit_section`'s `text`** reads that spelling back. A line `> **Info**` (the kind in any case, then nothing or a space and the title) **at the start of a block** opens that panel, and the panel runs for as long as the lines start with `>`. Its body is the lines with `>` and one space taken off, read by the section's own rules (`storage::from_plain`, which is also Confluence's `body_from_text`). It is written as `<ac:structured-macro ac:name="info" ac:schema-version="1">`, a `title` parameter when there is one, and `<ac:rich-text-body>`. Any other quote stays text, as typed. `body_from_text` (*New page*) is unchanged.
